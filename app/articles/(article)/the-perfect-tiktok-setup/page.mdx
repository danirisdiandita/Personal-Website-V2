Setting up a TikTok account that actually hits the US "For You Page" (FYP) when you're physically located elsewhere is a notorious challenge. I spent quite some time researching the right way to do it without getting shadowbanned or stuck in my local region's feed.

The common methods—just using a random VPN or changing the region in settings—simply don't work anymore. TikTok's detection is extremely sophisticated; they check your SIM card, GPS, and even the "cleanliness" of your device.

After trial and error, I found that the only reliable way is to treat the phone as a dedicated US device. I used a guide from VektaVPN and adapted it to my own workflow.

### The Problem with Most Setups
1. **SIM Detection**: If you have a local SIM card inserted, TikTok will ignore your VPN and lock you to your local region.
2. **IP Quality**: Using a public VPN IP often leads to instant shadowbans because thousands of others are using the same "dirty" IPs.
3. **Device Fingerprinting**: If the phone was previously used for a local account, traces remain even after a logout.

### My Step-by-Step Workflow
I used a spare iPhone exclusively for this. The key was to ensure the device never "knew" it was outside the US. I specifically used **VektaVPN** because it provides an unshared Residential IP, which is crucial to avoid being flagged as a "bot" or "VPN user".
